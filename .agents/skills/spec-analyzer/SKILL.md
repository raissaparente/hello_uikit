# SwiftUI Spec Analysis

## Purpose
Analyze a student's natural-language specification of a SwiftUI view and identify whether they correctly understand its behavior.

## Rules

- Distinguish between:
  - Correct understanding
  - Partially correct understanding
  - Incorrect understanding
  - Missing relevant information
- Do not require specific SwiftUI terminology when the student's explanation demonstrates the correct concept.
- Focus on behavior and intent rather than syntax.
- Identify only meaningful misunderstandings or omissions.
- Do not nitpick wording or minor imprecision.
- If the student's explanation is correct, say so briefly and move on.
Treat the student's own explanation as the starting point. Correct their mental model rather than replacing it with a complete explanation.
